# Template: License Clarification Request to Dehancer

Send through `dehancer.com/support` (or by email from the address on your account). Fill in the bracketed parts, keep the tone neutral, and save the reply. Written answers from the vendor are the strongest evidence of what your license includes.

---

**Subject:** License clarification — Dehancer Pro [lifetime / subscription], order [ORDER NUMBER]

Hello Dehancer team,

I purchased Dehancer Pro on [DATE] for $449 (order [ORDER NUMBER], account [ACCOUNT EMAIL]). I want to make sure I understand my license so I can keep my work and setup in good order. Could you please confirm the following in writing?

1. **License type.** Is my license a lifetime (perpetual) license or a subscription? How many seats and devices does it include?
2. **Governing terms.** Which edition (date) of the EULA and Terms of Service applies to my purchase? Which country's law governs them?
3. **Offline use.** After activation, does the plugin periodically re-check the license online? If so, how long can it run without an internet connection before it stops working or shows a watermark?
4. **Service continuity.** If Dehancer stops operating its activation servers or discontinues the product, what happens to lifetime licenses? Will lifetime holders receive an offline license file or a final activation-free build so the version they paid for keeps working?
5. **Legacy versions.** Will the version I'm using today stay activatable on my existing seats even after newer versions are released, including after a computer replacement?
6. **Exported LUTs.** May I use `.cube` LUTs exported with the LUT Generator:
   (a) in commercial client projects;
   (b) in other software (e.g., DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro);
   (c) after my license or subscription ends?
7. **Rendered output.** Is there any restriction on footage or images rendered with Dehancer after my license or subscription ends?
8. **Backups.** May I keep copies of the official installers for my own reinstallation?
9. **Exchange offers.** Please confirm that exchanging my lifetime license for a subscription would revoke the lifetime license, so I can decide carefully if I'm ever offered one.
10. **Transfer.** If I ever transfer my license to another person, what is the process, and does it carry the full lifetime term?

Thank you. A short written reply to each point would be very helpful for my records.

### Notes for the sender (don't include in the message)
- If you're in California, you can also ask: *"At checkout, was I shown a notice that 'buying' grants a license rather than ownership?"* This relates to Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code §17500.6 (AB 2426). Only ask if it's relevant to a dispute; it isn't needed for a routine clarification.
- If you have a **subscription**, also ask for: the renewal date and price, how to cancel online, and whether you'll get annual renewal reminders. California's Automatic Renewal Law, as amended by AB 2863, requires these for contracts entered, amended, or extended on or after July 1, 2025.
- Keep the reply with your invoice and the dated EULA PDF.
